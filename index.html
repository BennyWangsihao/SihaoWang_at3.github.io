Scroll Interaction Prototype

Scrollscape

A simple scroll-based prototype. Scroll down to move through different calm moods.

Morning Focus

Night Drift

This final section becomes darker and calmer. It gives the scroll journey a slow ending.

Prototype Concept

Scrollscape uses scroll as the main browser interaction. The user does not click buttons or move between pages. Instead, they scroll through different visual moods. The background, progress bar and text animation all respond to the user’s scroll position.

Sources

No external images, audio, fonts or JavaScript libraries are used. All visual elements are made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
